TELCO CUSTOMER CHURN: INSIGHTS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

KEY FINDINGS
1. Overall churn is 26.5%: 1,865 of 7,043 customers left, about 1 in 4.
2. Contract type is the biggest driver. Month-to-month customers churn at 44.2%, versus 8.3% for one-year and 1.3% for two-year contracts.
3. New customers leave the most. Churn is 51.2% in months 0-12, 37.4% in months 13-24, 17.1% in months 25-48, and 4.7% after 48 months.
4. Fiber optic customers churn more. Fiber is 36.3%, DSL is 21.3%, and customers with no internet are 14.4%.
5. Tech support helps retention. Among internet customers, those without it churn at 32.5% and those with it at 25.2%.
6. Payment method matters. Electronic check users churn at 40.8%, versus 17.2% for bank transfer, 20.1% for credit card and 22.8% for mailed check.
7. The riskiest combination is Month-to-month with Fiber optic, at 59.0% churn. New customers in that group churn at about 70%.
8. Churn costs about 137,600 per month, roughly 1.65 million per year in lost recurring revenue.

RECOMMENDATIONS
1. Month-to-month customers leave most (44.2%): offer a discount or free upgrade to move them to a one-year plan.
2. First-year customers leave most (51.2%): run a 90-day welcome program with check-in calls and usage tips.
3. Fiber customers without tech support leave more: bundle tech support and online security free for 3 months.
4. Electronic check users leave most (40.8%): give a small bill discount for switching to automatic payment.
5. Retention team needs a call list: use the At-Risk Customers page.

AT-RISK CUSTOMER LIST
The At-Risk page shows 847 current customers on month-to-month contracts with 12 months or less of tenure. In the same segment, 988 customers have already left, so about 54% of this segment (988 of 1,835) has churned. These 847 customers pay about 49,600 per month, roughly 595,500 per year, which is the revenue that early action could protect.

BUSINESS IMPACT ESTIMATE
Yearly revenue lost to churn = about 1,651,000.
If retention actions reduce churn by 10%, the company saves about 165,000 per year (1,651,000 x 0.10).
This is an estimate based on a 10% reduction assumption.

LIMITATIONS
- The dataset is synthetic, modelled on the public Telco churn structure, so the exact numbers are illustrative.
- These findings show associations, not proof of cause. A pilot test would be needed to show that discounts actually reduce churn.
